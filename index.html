    Host posts the rules

    Title, prize, number of winners and the rules in plain words. Stored on GenLayer, not on a spreadsheet.

  2. 2

    People enter with proof

    Each wallet enters once with a public link that shows they followed the rules.

  3. 3

    AI validators judge

    Several GenLayer validators open the link and must agree: valid, invalid, or unreadable.

  4. 4

    Fair draw, open receipt

    The host closes entries. Winners come from a SHA-256 seed of all valid entries — re-check it any time.
